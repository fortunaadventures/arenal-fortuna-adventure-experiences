Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

A few useful details to help you prepare for your adventure.

How do I check availability and current prices?

Send us an inquiry through the form or WhatsApp. Tour availability, rates and inclusions should be confirmed before booking.

Are the activities suitable for children?

Age, height, swimming ability and other requirements vary by activity. Please share the ages of everyone in your group so we can help you check the applicable requirements.

What should I bring?

Requirements vary by tour. Depending on your activity, you may need comfortable clothing, a change of clothes, sunscreen and appropriate footwear. We will confirm the specific instructions for your tour.

What happens if the weather changes?

Activities operate according to conditions and safety assessments. If conditions affect the safe operation of a tour, the operator will communicate the available options according to the applicable policy.

How do I make a reservation?

Contact us with your preferred date, number of guests and chosen activity. Your reservation is not confirmed until you receive confirmation from the relevant operator.

Before You Book

Cancellation Policy

Please review the applicable terms before confirming your reservation.

Reservation Changes and Cancellations

Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled activity may be eligible for a refund, less a 15% deduction for applicable administrative, payment-processing and transaction costs.

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the activity and no-shows are generally non-refundable under the stated booking policy.

Weather, river conditions and operational decisions may affect activities. Please contact the operator directly for information about changes, rescheduling or other available options.

If you booked through a third-party platform, contact that platform to manage your reservation, cancellation or refund request. The applicable booking terms and any mandatory legal rights remain relevant.
