Frequently asked questions

How do I confirm availability?

Send us your preferred date, activity and group size through WhatsApp. Your booking is confirmed only after availability and details have been verified.

Are the prices on this page final?

No. Prices are reference amounts and may change. Please confirm the current rate, taxes, inclusions and payment terms before booking.

Are there age or safety requirements?

Yes. Requirements vary by activity and conditions. Ask the operator about minimum age, swimming ability, physical requirements, equipment and safety instructions.

Are all activities operated by the same company?

Not necessarily. Some experiences may be provided by independent operators. Confirm the provider's identity, terms and meeting point before paying.
