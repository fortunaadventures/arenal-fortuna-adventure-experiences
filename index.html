Frequently asked questions

How do I confirm availability?

Send your preferred date, activity and group size through WhatsApp. Your booking is confirmed only after availability and details have been verified.

Are the prices on this page final?

No. Prices are reference amounts and may change. Confirm current rates, taxes, inclusions and payment terms before booking.

Are there age or safety requirements?

Requirements vary by activity and conditions. Ask the operator about age, swimming ability, physical requirements, equipment and safety instructions.

Are all activities operated by the same company?

Not necessarily. Some experiences may be provided by independent operators. Confirm the provider, terms and meeting point before paying.

Cancellation Policy

Please read this policy carefully before completing your reservation. These terms apply to reservations made through Arenal Adventure Experiences.

1. Standard Cancellation Policy

All cancellation requests must be submitted in writing. Eligibility is determined by the date and time the request is received by Arenal Adventure Experiences.

  • 24 hours or more before scheduled departure: refund less a 15% processing fee.
  • Less than 24 hours before departure: non-refundable.
  • No-shows are non-refundable.
  • Unused portions of a tour are non-refundable.

2. Why Cancellation Fees Apply

Reservations require advance planning and operational resources, including payment processing, commissions, administration, guide scheduling, transport, meals and safety equipment. Some costs are committed before departure and cannot be recovered after cancellation.

3. Strict Time-Based Policy — No Exceptions

Cancellations received less than 24 hours before departure are non-refundable and are not eligible for credits or rescheduling, regardless of the reason, subject to applicable mandatory legal rights.

This includes illness, medical emergencies, flight delays or cancellations, missed connections, traffic, road closures, transportation failures, weather affecting personal travel, family emergencies, work conflicts, passport or visa issues, hotel problems, lost or delayed baggage, cruise changes or other personal or travel-related circumstances.

Guests must arrive at the designated meeting point at the specified check-in time. No-shows, late arrivals or travel disruptions do not exempt guests from this policy.

4. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are generally processed within seven (7) business days after review and approval. The time for funds to appear in an account varies by bank, card issuer or payment provider.

5. Acceptance of Terms

By completing a reservation, guests acknowledge that they have read and accepted this policy and applicable booking terms, subject to mandatory legal rights.

6. Policy Administration

Arenal Adventure Experiences is responsible for establishing, communicating and applying the cancellation and refund policies stated on this website, subject to applicable mandatory legal requirements.
